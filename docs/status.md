# Status: SimulatoKit 0.8.1 <!-- x-release-please-version -->

Against abTEM 1.0.10. Live: crystal cells and the projected potential from the Lobato or Kirkland parameterisation (`project`, tiled; slices that follow the crystal planes), a specimen from an atom model (the beam along x, y or z, vacuum padding as ASE's `center`), a general crystal cell turned to a zone axis and cut into an orthogonal slab (`CrystalCell`, `SlabBuilder`: abTEM's `orthogonalize_cell` with a strain limit, slices that repeat with the crystal, atomic planes kept off slice boundaries; the intensity of each zone-axis beam by the crystal's hkl at every stored thickness, `BeamIntensities`), any grid length of at least 2 (vDSP where it has
the length, a mixed-radix FFT for 2, 3, 5 and 7 and a generic odd-radix pass for the other lengths, Bluestein where a large prime factor makes it the cheaper), multislice on `.cpu` (float64) and `.metal` (float32, held to the CPU), beam tilt, the probe with
polar aberrations C10 to C56, `GridScan`, frozen phonons (σ per element or per site, σ ↔ B; exit waves too; partly occupied sites drawn per configuration), annular, flexible annular (radial bins summed to any band after the run), pixelated, segmented (DPC: integrate, integrateRadial, differentials on a polar scan) and centre-of-mass detectors, `STEM.scan`, the contrast transfer function and HRTEM image series over defocus and thickness,
source size and Poisson noise (samples too), `LineScan` (matched to a probe: end and Nyquist sampling defaults) and line profiles, integrated centre of mass (`integrate_gradient`, iCOM), the azimuthal average of a centred pattern and of every pattern of a pixelated scan, Fourier-space image interpolation and `integrateDisc(interpolate:)`, image crop and relative difference, `WavesDetector` (the exit wave at every scan position), and progress and cancellation in multislice runs and scans, Metal runs committed in chunks (two runs interleave) and `STEMRun`, a scan that stops and continues, and `SimulationPlan` (sampling, grid, memory and time before a run, refusing what does not fit). What is checked against what: `docs/parity.md` (48 present, 44 partial, 45 missing of 137 rows); timings against Python:
`docs/benchmarks.md`; what is open: the GitHub issues; known limits: `docs/parity.md`.

Since 0.5.0: 0.6.0 adds the crystal cell and its slab along a zone axis, the Kirkland parameterisation, beam intensities by hkl, HRTEM image series on `.cpu` and `.metal`, the transfer function profiles, `SimulationPlan`, progress and cancellation, thermal vibration per site, the Gaussian filter, and image crop and relative difference. Two changes break source: the public initialisers throw `invalidShape` where they trapped (add `try`), and `parameterization:` takes a `Parameterization` (`.lobato(...)`, `.kirkland(...)`); the Migration paragraphs are in pull requests #82 and #79. Details: `CHANGELOG.md`.

Tests are split in two. `SimulatoKitParityTests` holds the pinned abTEM comparisons, the truth tests, Metal against CPU and the benchmarks (127 tests; fixtures live in its `Fixtures/`); the other targets hold the fast unit tests (99 tests); `SimulatoKitTestSupport` holds the helpers both use. `swift test -c release --parallel --skip SimulatoKitParityTests` runs the fast half; the plain `swift test -c release --parallel` runs all 226.
